VHLS Algorithms

Vandenbergh-Harris Last Slot: orient the last-layer edges while inserting the final F2L pair. 32 cases, grouped by pair position. Hold the cube with the empty slot at FR.

Diagrams follow the Speedsolving Wiki VHLS page format. Algorithms from Sarah's Cubing Site (originally by Jasom Baum).
