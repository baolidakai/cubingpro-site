CFOP Method

CFOP — Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL — is the most popular method for speedsolving the 3×3×3. You solve the cube in four stages, from the bottom up. Almost every world-class speedcuber uses CFOP.

Méthode CFOP

CFOP — Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL — est la méthode la plus populaire pour résoudre le 3×3×3 en vitesse. On résout le cube en quatre étapes, de bas en haut. Presque tous les speedcubers de classe mondiale utilisent CFOP.

Metodo CFOP

CFOP — Cross, F2L, OLL, PLL — è il metodo più popolare per risolvere il 3×3×3 in velocità. Si risolve il cubo in quattro fasi, dal basso verso l'alto. Quasi tutti gli speedcuber di classe mondiale usano CFOP.

1. Cross

Solve the four bottom edges to form a cross. Plan it entirely during inspection and execute in 8 moves or fewer. Everything else builds on a good cross.

2. F2L — First Two Layers

Pair each corner with its matching edge and insert the four pairs into their slots. 41 standard cases. F2L takes up most of your solve time — the stage where lookahead and efficiency pay off the most.

3. OLL — Orient Last Layer

Orient every last-layer piece so the top face becomes a single color. 57 cases, one algorithm each.

OLL algorithms →

4. PLL — Permute Last Layer

Permute the last-layer pieces into their solved positions. 21 cases, one algorithm each.

PLL algorithms →

How to learn

Suggested order: Cross → F2L (intuitive first, full algorithms later) → PLL → OLL. You don’t need all 119 algorithms to start — a 4-look last layer (2-look OLL + 2-look PLL) gets you most of the way there.
