5 BLD trainer

There are three trainers available:

  1. First trainer helps you practice memo only.
  2. Second trainer helps you practice tracing only.
  3. Third trainer helps you practice execution only.

Prerequisite: You should already know how to solve 5 BLD, and have a letter scheme for it.

Provide your letter scheme by either choosing from the two presets.

For all trainers, you will provided a scramble generated from a timer or other online resources.

Note: Scramble from your regular cube holding position.

For the memo practice trainer, you should memorize the whole cube using your standard memo order, then after you memorize, enter your letter pairs (including the last parity letter).

The trainer will score correctness for each of the piece types (e.g. wings, corners) individually.

The tracing trainer is similar to the memo trainer, but you may enter the memo while tracing directly, so you don't have to memorize the pieces but instead just go through them quickly.

For the execution trainer, you will be provided also an auto-generated memo sequence. You will be responsible for executing acording to the memo sequence, and see whether you got successful.
