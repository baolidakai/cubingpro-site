    How to use this cube timer

    Hold SPACE (or press and hold the timer on a phone) until it turns green, then release to start. If inspection is on, you get the standard 15 seconds to look at the cube first — press SPACE again to start solving. Press SPACE again (or tap) to stop. Press ESC to cancel. Your times are saved in your browser automatically, so your session is still here when you come back.

    Random-move scrambles — read this first

    Scrambles on this page are random moves, not random-state scrambles. A 3x3 scramble here is 20 random turns (2x2: 11, 4x4: 40, 5x5: 60), with no face repeated twice in a row. That is the classic, easy way to scramble — the same kind most timers have used for years. It is not a WCA random-state scramble (a scramble generated from a random cube state, which guarantees a uniform distribution and usually needs a server or a heavy algorithm). For practice and timing yourself, random-move scrambles are perfectly fine. We label them honestly so you know exactly what you are solving.

    What do Ao5 and Ao12 mean?

    Ao5 is the average of your last 5 solves, with the best and worst times removed. Ao12 is the same over your last 12. These trimmed averages are how speedcubers track real progress — one lucky or unlucky solve does not move them much. Click +2 on a solve to add the 2-second inspection penalty, DNF if the solve did not finish, or × to delete it. Averages follow WCA rules: one DNF is dropped as the worst time, two or more make the average a DNF.

    FAQ

    Is this Rubik's Cube timer free? Yes — free, no account, no install. It runs entirely in your browser.

    Is this a csTimer alternative? It is inspired by csTimer's way of working (hold SPACE to start, scramble on top, session averages), but it is a simpler, lighter timer built for this site: fewer settings to get lost in, and the scramble method is stated up front.

    Does it work on mobile? Yes. Press and hold the big timer to arm it, release to start, and tap to stop.

    Where are my times stored? In your browser's local storage, on your device only. Clearing your browser data clears your session. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    Which events are supported? 2x2, 3x3, 4x4 and 5x5, all with random-move scrambles.
