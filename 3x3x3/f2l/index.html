Algoritmi F2L

Tutti i 41 casi F2L (primi due strati) con un algoritmo veloce e diffuso per ciascuno, raggruppati per posizione di angolo e spigolo. I diagrammi evidenziano solo i due pezzi rilevanti.

The Two Standard Inserts

Almost every F2L case is solved by first converting it into one of these two basic patterns, then doing the standard 3-move insert. Learn these two by heart — everything else builds on them.

R U R'
The basic 3-move insert. Pair is formed at FR — R lifts, U aligns, R' drops it in.
U R U' R'
Same insert with a U setup first. Use when the pair needs one U turn to line up.
