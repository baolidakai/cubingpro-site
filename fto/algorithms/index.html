FTO Algorithms

The full LowCubes L3T algorithm sets for the Face-Turning Octahedron: PF, TL, LT, TCP and 1L3T — 216 cases in total. Learn them in order: 3-Look L3T is PF → TL → LT, 2-Look L3T is PF → TCP, and 1-Look L3T solves the last three triangles in a single look.

Algorithms by LowCubes / Raul Low — copied from cuberoot.me/alg/fto (LowCubes FTO · PF/TL/LT video · TCP/1L3T video)

38 of the 298 algorithms below failed our simulator check — setup + algorithm does not solve its case — and are marked Unverified. Do not learn them yet; they are kept here for reference until replacements are found.

Notation — LowCubes EIF

  • No suffix means a 120° clockwise turn as viewed straight at that face; a prime (') reverses it. On face, wide and slice moves, 2 means two successive 120° turns.
  • Face turns: U, F, R, L, D, Bl, Br and B turn one triangular face. Bl and Br are the back-left and back-right faces.
  • Wide and slice turns: w turns the face and its adjacent middle layer together; s turns only the corresponding middle layer. Uw Fw Rw Lw Dw Blw Brw Bw — Us Fs Rs Ls.
  • Whole-puzzle rotations: Uo, Fo, Ro and Lo rotate 120° around the corresponding face axis. Rt, Lt and Ft rotate 90° around the corresponding vertex axis; a prime reverses it and 2 means 180°.
  • Macros: S and H are common macros in Bencisco algorithms.
    • S = R' L R L'
    • S' = L R' L' R
    • H = R U2 R2 U
    • H' = U2 R U R2
